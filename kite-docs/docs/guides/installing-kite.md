# Installing Kite (PWA)

Kite can be installed as a Progressive Web App (PWA) on desktop and mobile devices. Installing Kite provides a standalone window, a launcher icon on your home screen or desktop, and a more seamless experience.

:::warning Experimental & Partial Support

PWA and mobile support is currently experimental and not fully supported.

:::

## Installing on Desktop

### Google Chrome & Microsoft Edge

1. Open [kite.onl](https://kite.onl) in Chrome or Edge.
2. Look for the **Install** icon on the right side of the address bar:
   - **Chrome**: Click the computer/download icon in the URL bar.
   - **Edge**: Click the **App available** icon in the URL bar.
3. Alternatively, open the browser menu (**⋮** or **…**) and select **Install Kite**.
4. Click **Install** in the confirmation dialog.

Kite will open in a standalone window and appear in your operating system's app launcher.

---

## Installing on Mobile

### Android (Google Chrome)

1. Open [kite.onl](https://kite.onl) in Chrome.
2. Tap the **three-dot menu (⋮)** in the top-right corner.
3. Select **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
4. Tap **Install** in the confirmation prompt.

The Kite icon will be added to your home screen and app drawer.

### iOS (Safari)

Apple requires manual installation through Safari's Share sheet:

1. Open [kite.onl](https://kite.onl) in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an upward arrow at the bottom of the screen).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.

Kite will appear on your home screen and launch in standalone mode.
