# Kite Concepts for Discord Bot Building

Welcome to Kite, an open-source no-code platform to build and manage Discord bots. Here, we'll break down the core concepts of Kite, empowering you to create dynamic and interactive bots without any programming knowledge.

## Commands

### What Are Commands?

Commands are pre-defined actions your bot can perform on Discord, typically triggered by users. When a user types a specific keyword or phrase, your bot listens for this and responds as designed.

### When to Use Commands

As a user, you'll interact with commands when you want your bot to perform a specific task like sending a welcome message, fetching user information, or setting reminders. Setting up commands allows your bot to understand and act on various triggers provided by the community or server members.

## Message Templates

### What Are Message Templates?

Message Templates are predefined formats that your bot uses to send structured messages to your server. They include the content, embeds, attachments, and interactive components like buttons your bot will send as responses.

### When to Use Message Templates

Use message templates when you want to keep your bot's responses consistent and structured. They are perfect for announcements, alerts, informative embeds, or any recurring communication your bot sends.

## Event Listeners

### What Are Event Listeners?

Event Listeners are set up to monitor specific activities or events in your Discord server. Once an event occurs—like a message being sent or a user joining—the event listener triggers a predefined set of actions.

### When to Use Event Listeners

You'll configure event listeners when you need your bot to react to server events. For instance, use them to greet new members, log messages, or track specific behaviors directly tied to Discord server dynamics.

## Flows

### What Are Flows?

Flows in Kite represent the logic paths your bot follows to execute commands or respond to events. They define the sequence of operations based on inputs and decisions, crafting the bot's operational structure.

### When to Work with Flows

You'll engage with flows when designing the logic for your bot's actions and reactions. Think of flows as blueprints that chart out how data moves through commands and events to deliver specific outcomes.

## Plugins

### What Are Plugins?

Plugins in Kite extend the capabilities of your bot, adding pre-packaged functionalities that integrate smoothly into existing systems. Each plugin comes with specific features, like interaction handling or event augmentations, increasing your bot's versatility without custom coding.

### When to Use Plugins

Select plugins when your bot requires capabilities beyond standard setups, like integrating a counting game or a reaction-based leaderboard (starboard). They allow for quick enhancements to your bot's skillset without needing to start from scratch.

## Variables

### What Are Variables?

Variables store temporary data that your bot can access and manipulate across different flows and actions. Think of them as placeholders that hold dynamic information your bot might need to reference or modify.

### When to Use Variables

You'll use variables when your bot needs to track or remember information—like a user's progress within a game or the state of an ongoing event—that changes over time or between interactions.

## Apps

### What Are Apps?

Apps in Kite represent distinct bot instances labeled by unique identifiers. Each app is essentially an independent bot configuration, working under its own set of rules, commands, and plugins.

### When to Use Apps

You'll consider apps when managing multiple bots or needing different configurations for various communities or purposes. Apps help in segregating distinct functionalities and ensuring each bot operates within its designed parameters.

By understanding these concepts, you'll be empowered to create and customize your Discord bots effectively without touching a single line of code. Get creative and explore the limitless possibilities Kite provides for enhancing your Discord community!
